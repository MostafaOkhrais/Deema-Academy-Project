# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, no UI framework), Supabase for accounts, TypeScript for data and scripts, scoped component CSS over shared tokens. Deploy target: Vercel (static). Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Arabic-speaking students and recent graduates in health sciences (pharmacy first; also medicine, nursing, labs, nutrition) who sense the traditional career path is too narrow and want options in digital health, AI tools, research and medical content. They arrive from Instagram posts and reels, usually on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Deema Academy is a remote Arabic learning platform that joins health sciences, AI, scientific research and medical content creation. Success is a visitor registering for a track or an event and later building a professional identity beyond the standard pharmacy/clinical job.

## Positioning

Taught by one recognisable teacher-persona, Dr. Deema (a pharmacist, health educator and content creator), in an approachable-but-rigorous voice. A neighbouring course site could not copy the persona, the notebook-paper identity or the "your major is the beginning, not the limit" stance.

## Operating Context

Learning is remote: recorded lectures, live workshops and meetings on Zoom, one-to-one guidance, research and freelance mentoring. Content is in Arabic, right-to-left.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five tracks: clinical pharmacy practice; AI and digital health; medical content creation; academic research and writing; professional identity and remote work.
- Four learning formats: digital learning, explanation and guidance, meetings and workshops, research and freelance.
- Accounts: email + password (Supabase), sign-up, login, password reset; each member has a profile, enrollments (tracks and the free workshop, status pending/approved/rejected/completed) and certificates; an admin page manages requests, members and certificates. Roles and permissions are enforced by row level security.
- Undecided: real event schedule, certificates, pricing, team names, social links, domain (board shows deema.academy and @deema.academy).

## Brand Commitments

Visual identity board (`C:\Users\Mostafa\Desktop\Deema_Academy`) is binding:
- Palette: black #000000, sky blue #91C8E0, academic navy #002147, soft grey #F2F2F2, white #FFFFFF.
- Display face: Thmanyah Serif Display (official, free, but files may not be redistributed; the user downloads them from Thmanyah). Fallback is a self-hosted Arabic naskh serif.
- Character Dr. Deema is used as approved, without modification. Logo is a person-and-drop mark with a sparkle; lockups per board.
- Notebook graph-paper background is the identity's post ground.
- Tagline: "فكّر خارج الصندوق.. تخصصك هو البداية، مش حدود مستقبلك".

## Evidence on Hand

Logo, character sheet and approved poses (cropped to `src/assets`). Absent and not to be invented: testimonials, student counts, partner names, certificate claims, prices, real team members. Event dates and FAQ answers are placeholders to be confirmed by the owner.

## Product Principles

1. Rigour first, warmth in delivery: every claim is checkable and modest.
2. One teacher, one voice: the character leads, not stock imagery.
3. Show the path: each track names what you learn and where it can lead.
4. Phone first: most visitors arrive from social.
5. Never promise jobs or certificates the academy has not defined.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic RTL first; text contrast at least 4.5:1; keyboard operable; reduced-motion respected; works at 320px width.
