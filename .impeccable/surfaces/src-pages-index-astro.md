# Surface brief: Deema Academy landing (Persuade)

Scope: single landing page; visitor registers for the free workshop or a track. Mode: Persuade. World pinned by the owner's real Instagram post system (graph-paper white posts, navy grid posts, sky icon art). No direction tournament: the owner's own post language is binding.

Audience/job: Arabic-speaking health-science students/graduates, mostly on phones from Instagram. Proof: Dr. Deema's real story (Palestine University 2019, top ten, Anera, freelance, AI engineering), the real free workshop. Constraints: Astro static, RTL, Thmanyah Serif Display + Sans (user's licensed copy).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the academy's post system scaled to a page: one heavy Thmanyah headline per screen on graph paper or navy grid, a black label tag, grey rounded numbered rows, a sky pill action. It refuses the SaaS template: equal icon cards, floating badges, gradients, glass, soft shadows.
OWN-WORLD: 36px graph-paper ground (sky hairlines), navy grid surfaces, solid sky fields; Thmanyah Serif Display Black headlines with one sky-highlighted word on navy; Thmanyah Sans body; line icons (2px navy/sky, round caps) as in the post icon art; grey #F2F2F2 rows with outlined number circles; black pill tag; sky pill CTA; large 28-32px radii like the posts.
STORY: Visitor meets Dr. Deema and the stance (your major is the beginning), reads her real path, picks a track, sees the free workshop, follows three steps and registers.
